held across both halves of 27 years.

THE MECHANISM IS AN EXPLANATION ATTACHED AFTERWARDS. It is
economics, not output: nothing here was fitted and no sentence was
tested. It is included because a ranking without a reason invites
the reader to invent one, and a stated reason can be argued with.

The sentences are COMPOSED from declared currency roles plus the
pair's measured position in the ranking, so 28 sentences cannot
quietly disagree with 28 numbers.
