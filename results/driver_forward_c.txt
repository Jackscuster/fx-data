Driver C -- forward odds over the next 20 bars
==============================================

FORWARD, and reported as its own block. Layer 1 does not predict --
only drivers carry forward odds, and nothing here changes a state
label.

P(crisis in 20 bars) is computed at DAY level against distinct
event dates: ONE CRISIS IS ONE OBSERVATION however many bars or
pairs it touches. Regime odds are per pair -- does the pair enter
that state at any point in the next 20 bars.

base is the unconditional rate in that block; lift is bucket over
base. Terciles cut on in-sample only.
