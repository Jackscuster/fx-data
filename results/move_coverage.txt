MOVE coverage
=============

^MOVE runs 2002-11-12 to 2026-07-31. The FX sample starts 1999-01-04,
so the first ~3.9 years have no MOVE at all and IS is a 13-year window
rather than 17. Unit of observation is a DAY, not a pair-day, because
MOVE is one global series hitting all 28 pairs simultaneously.
