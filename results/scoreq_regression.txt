The guardrail: the regression suite the challenger had to pass
==============================================================

The in-sample procedure chose 0.40 over the shipped 0.50, so by the
declared guardrail nothing could ship until the full suite passed:
refit control, agreement against shipped overall and per state, run
lengths, flip rate, and a null test on separation.

IT DID NOT PASS. On the holdout, measured on identical rows, the
challenger is WORSE on both axes: trend 0.1324 against 0.1354 and chop
0.1130 against 0.1368. The in-sample gain of +0.0085 reverses to -0.0224.

So 0.50 stays, and the outcome is recorded as TESTED, MEDIAN
SURVIVED -- which is a stronger position than the one this started
from, because the median is now a decision with a criterion and a
failed challenger behind it rather than a convention.
