Chronic currency episodes, dated from the news record
=====================================================

Every start and end date is a policy announcement, meeting outcome
or intervention -- something reported on that day. Dating a
"sustained depreciation" from where the chart started falling would
make the detector's validation worthless, because the detector
reads the chart. This is the same principle that made the 54-event
acute calendar non-circular.

6 rows, 5 INDEPENDENT macro events.

THE OVERLAP MATTERS. EUR-down 2014-15 and USD-up 2014-15 are the
same divergence seen from two sides. They are listed separately
because the detector runs per currency, but any count of
independent episodes must use the macro_event column, not the row
count.

SIX ROWS IS A SMALL SAMPLE AND FIVE INDEPENDENT EVENTS IS SMALLER.
What that costs: no in-sample/out-of-sample split of the episode
list is meaningful -- three of the six start before 2016 and three
after, so a split leaves two or three events a side. Separation can
be measured and null-tested; a holdout confirmation on the episode
list cannot. That limit is stated here rather than discovered in
the results.

GBP 2016 is the least clean member: it begins with a shock, so it
is part acute. Kept and flagged rather than quietly dropped.
