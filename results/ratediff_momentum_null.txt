Null -- circular shift of yields against price
==============================================

The yield panel is rolled by a random offset of at least 1000 bars and
the whole statistic recomputed. Both series keep their own internal
behaviour and only the ALIGNMENT between them is broken, which is the
thing being tested.

n_shifts is the exact draw count actually used, not a target.
rank_of_real is where the real number sits among (nulls + itself),
1 being the largest.
