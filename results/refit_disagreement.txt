Are refit disagreements boundary noise or structural drift?
===========================================================

Two different failures look identical in an agreement rate. Scattered
single days are BOUNDARY NOISE -- bars sitting on a cut point, which is
unavoidable and harmless. Whole episodes relabelled are STRUCTURAL
DRIFT -- the classifier telling a different story about the same
stretch of market, which is not harmless.

Runs are contiguous disagreeing bars within one pair. An episode is a
run of one shipped state on one pair, at least 5 bars, wholly after the
vintage date; it counts as fully relabelled only if EVERY one of its
bars disagrees.

READ THE RUN LENGTHS AGAINST THE DWELL. The classifier requires 5
consecutive bars before adopting a new state, so a relabelling cannot
be one bar wide by construction and the share of bars sitting in runs
of 5 or more is partly forced. The median run of 3-4 bars is BELOW the
dwell, which is the informative part: most disagreement is shorter than
the confirmation window, i.e. edge-of-threshold noise rather than the
classifier adopting a different story.
