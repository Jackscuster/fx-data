Driver F -- does CFTC positioning separate the states?
======================================================

THE LAG, which is what fakes results here. A COT report is TUESDAY
positions published FRIDAY afternoon, treated as usable from the
following MONDAY: report_date + 6 calendar days, then the standard
one-bar shift. Seven calendar days from snapshot to first usable bar.
A reading is dropped once older than 12 days, so NZD does not get its
final 2022 report carried to 2026.

COVERAGE. CME FX futures are quoted against USD, so this reaches 7 of
28 pairs and no cross at all. NZD stops being reported after
2022-02-01.

SIGNED OR ABSOLUTE, declared before running. State labels carry no
direction -- "trending" covers up and down -- so a signed position
averaged over trending episodes cancels toward zero by construction.
Separation is therefore decided on the ABSOLUTE readings (F1 crowding,
F2 turnover); the signed ones (F3, F4) are reported beside them so the
choice can be checked, and are not decisive.

Episode-based: one state run is one observation. Circular-shift null,
exact draw count in n_shifts. Sub-period split ran before any holdout
pass was reported.
