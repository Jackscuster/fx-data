The strongest and weakest cells of the agreement matrix
=======================================================

Every one of the strongest cells shares a currency leg, which is the
rank-7 identity showing up rather than a discovery. Note that sharing a
leg does NOT guarantee agreement -- GBPCAD/USDCAD is among the WEAKEST
cells on the holdout at -0.157 despite both carrying CAD.

Individual cells repeat only moderately between halves (spearman 0.37),
so this table is a description of structure, not a lookup table.
