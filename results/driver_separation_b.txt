Driver B -- does MOVE separate the day types?
=============================================

POOLED BY DAY. MOVE is one global series, so a day is one observation
however many of the 28 pairs sit in a given state. Pooling by pair
would let one world event count 28 times.

Day types: crisis (forward-only window, event date to +15 bars), then
among the rest, trend-leaning if the trending share exceeds the ranging
share and range-leaning otherwise.

sep_level and sep_chg are the group mean minus every other day, in sd
units of that block.
