Question 2 -- lead into trending
================================

At each transition INTO trending, the differential momentum read at the
bar BEFORE the change. Momentum is already lagged one bar, so that value
uses yields through t-2 -- strictly before the bar the state changes on.

Direction is the sign of the price move over the trending episode that
follows, which is forward-looking BY DESIGN: this is the lead test, and
it is reported separately from question 1 for exactly that reason.

The control draws non-transition bars with the same forward horizon and
reads them identically, three per transition.
