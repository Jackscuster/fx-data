Chronic detector -- separation and boundaries
=============================================

Groups: chronic (news-dated windows, affected pairs), TRENDING BUT NOT
CHRONIC -- the hard boundary, because chronic IS a trend -- acute (the
54-event forward windows) and everything else.

Rows tagged "chronic IS" / "chronic OOS" split the BARS. The EPISODE
LIST cannot be split: 3 of 6 rows start before 2016 and 3 after, which
leaves 2-3 independent events a side. No holdout claim is made.

The null circularly shifts the episode WINDOWS in time, preserving
their number and length, and recomputes the separation.
