Question 1 -- present-tense association
=======================================

One row per (window, block, state). EPISODE-BASED: one state run is one
observation, so a 40-bar trending episode contributes a single
agree/disagree rather than forty.

agree  = share of episodes where sign(price move over the episode)
         matches sign(mean differential momentum during it)
base_all_states = the same share pooled over episodes of EVERY state,
         which is the honest baseline -- 0.50 is not, because price and
         yields both drift
excess = agree minus base

W was chosen on IS only (W=5) and the holdout read once.
