What is refit, what is fixed, and what was selected once
========================================================

Refit stability means nothing until it says which numbers move. 394
numbers are estimated from data and move with the vintage; the window
lengths, dwell, hysteresis band, equal weights and cell boundaries are
fixed by construction and move with nothing.

THE CAVEAT, STATED RATHER THAN BURIED: DROP_TESTS and BUMP were chosen
by in-sample comparison and are held fixed across vintages. This
measures the stability of the ESTIMATED parameters, not of the
SELECTION decisions. Re-running those choices at every vintage is a
larger test and is not claimed here.
