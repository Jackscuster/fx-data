CFTC COT coverage and the release lag
=====================================

A COT report gives positions as of TUESDAY close and is published
the following FRIDAY at 15:30 ET. Each report is mapped here to the
first FX bar on or after report_date + 6 calendar days -- the
following Monday -- held until the next report, then shifted one
further bar like every other signal in this project.

Effective lag from position snapshot to first usable bar: SEVEN
calendar days. Using the report date directly would look like a
five-day head start on a weekly series, which is exactly the error
that manufactures a finding.

CME FX futures are quoted against USD only, so COT reaches 7 of 28
pairs: EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, USDJPY. Every cross has no positioning data at all.

SIGN: COT reports the FOREIGN contract, so a long JPY future is
short USDJPY. For USDxxx pairs the net is negated, so a positive
reading always means speculators are long the pair as quoted here.
