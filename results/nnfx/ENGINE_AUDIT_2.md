## 1. Fill price, and a correction to the question

Jack asked for "17:00 close versus the real 22:00 price". The premise needs correcting first, and the correction is in his favour.


The daily feed Layer 2 trades is `data/oanda_ohlc`, **not** the 17:00 New York file (`data/px28_5pm.csv`, from `build5pm.py`) that HANDOFF describes for Layer 1. `nnfxamb.py` measured which window the daily bar actually spans by scanning every candidate cut, and the bar dated D is best matched by **(16:00 NY on D−1, 16:00 NY on D]** — 72.8% containment against 66.5% at 15:00 and 62.8% at 17:00. So the engine is not filling at a 17:00 price, and what a 22:00 price would do to it is not the live question.


The question underneath is the one worth answering: **is the close the engine fills at a price that existed at the moment it claims?** Mean gap between the daily close and the hourly mid at each candidate hour, converted to R at that bar's ATR:

## 2. Overnight financing

**It is not charged. Anywhere.** Checked rather than remembered: `grep -niE "carry|swap|financ|overnight|rollover|interest"` over `l2engine.py` and `l2sweep.py` returns only the English word "carry" in three comments and one "carries" about data labels. There is no financing term in the fill path, the cost path or the scorer.


**The size, with the caveat first.** `data/rates2y.csv` holds two-year yields, not overnight rates, and a broker charges a spread on the swap on top of the interbank differential. So this is a proxy: the sign is right, the shape across pairs and years is right, and the level is a **lower bound** on what a real account would pay.

Mean financing per trade across these candidates is **+0.00146 R** against a mean spread charge of 0.01804 R, a ratio of 0.85 in absolute size. 37% of trades would PAY financing and the rest would be paid it.


The sign is the part that matters. Mean financing is POSITIVE, so adding it would FLATTER these candidates. A trend strategy long the carry currencies in a carry-friendly decade is flattered by financing, not hurt, and leaving it out is therefore the conservative choice here — which is the opposite of what it looks like.
