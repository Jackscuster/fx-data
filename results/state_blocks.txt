Does agreement cluster around a shared currency leg?
====================================================

Each currency block is the 7 pairs containing that currency. A pair
belongs to TWO blocks, so blocks overlap: "within" and "across" are not
a partition of the pairs but of the 378 PAIRS-OF-PAIRS, by whether the
two share a leg.

Read this against the rank-7 floor. Pairs sharing a leg are
algebraically linked, so a higher figure inside a block is expected and
the informative quantity is the GAP between share-leg and no-leg.
