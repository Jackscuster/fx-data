Test 2 -- does agreement add confidence?
========================================

Driver A: trending episodes split at the in-sample median driver size.
"Agrees" means the rate gap was moving hard while the state was
trending. The question is whether the state CALL is better there --
longer runs -- not whether price did anything.

Driver B: daily flip rate, the share of the 28 pairs changing state
that day, on high-MOVE against low-MOVE days. Pooled by day.

This is a confidence input for sizing later. It is NOT a new state and
nothing here changes a label.
