Pre-1999 coverage
=================

The brief assumed 21 pairs over 1990-1998. It is 3, and the
reason is the yields, not the prices.

FX is obtainable in full: H.10 is mirrored on FRED as the DEX*
series back to 1971 and FRED is reachable. The Fed download
endpoint returns 403 from here, which is why the repo copy of
px28.csv starts 1999-01-04.

2-year yields pre-1999:
  USD  FRED DGS2, 1976 onward                 AVAILABLE
  GBP  BoE GLC zip, 1979 onward               AVAILABLE
  CHF  SNB cache, 1988 onward                 AVAILABLE
  JPY  MoF cache parses to zero rows          NOT AVAILABLE
  CAD  Bank of Canada cache starts 2001-02-15 NOT AVAILABLE
  AUD  RBA current cache is 31 rows           NOT AVAILABLE
  NZD  no data anywhere in the repo           NOT AVAILABLE

AND THE RUNNABLE PAIRS ARE NOT INDEPENDENT. Three currencies
give three pairs and any one is the ratio of the other two --
GBPCHF is GBPUSD divided by USDCHF exactly. The effective
sample is nearer two independent series than three.
