Holdout separation, split by sub-period
=======================================

Both drivers scored their strongest separation on the HOLDOUT rather
than in-sample, which is unusual enough to check before reporting. The
holdout contains COVID and the 2022 rate cycle, so a result
concentrated there is a result about two years, not about the sample.

Driver A is NOT robust: the ranging separation is +0.160 in 2016-19,
the WRONG SIGN, and only turns negative from 2020. Its holdout result
is a post-COVID effect.

Driver B IS robust: range-leaning is -0.538 / -0.314 / -0.648 across
the three sub-periods and crisis is +0.932 / n-a / +0.852. Same sign,
similar size, in every one.
