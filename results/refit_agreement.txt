Refit stability -- do the state calls survive re-estimation?
============================================================

Every fitted quantity is re-derived from data ending at each vintage
year and the whole state history is regenerated for all 28 pairs. The
shipped classifier fits on index < 2016-01-01, so the 2015 vintage is
the shipped fit and MUST reproduce it exactly. The run asserts that;
a refit test that cannot reproduce its own starting point is measuring
its own plumbing.

Two scopes are reported. "All overlapping days" includes days inside
the vintage's own fit window. "POST-VINTAGE ONLY" is the number that
matters -- it is the live case, where a cut point estimated on the past
is applied to data it never saw.

Agreement is reported beside the agreement expected from the two
marginal distributions alone, and kappa corrects for it. Four states
unevenly distributed agree ~27% of the time by coincidence.

Worst post-vintage agreement: 0.939 (vintage 2024).
