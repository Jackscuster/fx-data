Today -- the market-wide header
===============================

Breadth is how many of the 28 pairs share the dominant state. The floor
is 7, not 1: four states over 28 pairs means the largest group cannot
be smaller than ceil(28/4). A median day sits near 11.

MOVE is the bond-volatility index, the ONE external driver of six that
survived testing. It is a witness on the present, not a forecast: it
reads about 0.9 sd higher on crisis days in every sub-period, and every
attempt to make it predict failed.

acute_crisis_window is whether today falls inside a forward-only window
from a news-dated event in the 54-event calendar. The window opens ON
the event date and never before it.
