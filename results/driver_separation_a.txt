Driver A -- does the SIZE of the rate-gap move separate the states?
===================================================================

Driver is |differential - its value 21 bars ago|, lagged one bar. SIZE,
not sign: the question is whether the gap is moving hard.

Episode-based: one state run is one observation. An episode counts as
crisis if any of its bars falls in a forward-only window from an event
date to +15 bars (54 events in events.py).

sep_vs_rest is the group mean minus every other group, in sd units of
the driver. Null rows carry the exact shift count.
