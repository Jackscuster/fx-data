Window sensitivity -- do nearby lookbacks give a different machine?
===================================================================

THIS IS NOT A RE-TUNE. The shipped lookback stays at 106 bars. Nothing
here is selected, no window is compared on separation or any other
quality measure, and no window is proposed. 90 and 120 were declared
before running, chosen only as "near", and the measure is the SAME
per-day agreement the refit test uses so the two read against each
other.

WHAT THE LOOKBACK TOUCHES: disp (net/path), tests, inside, revert. What
it does not: fails (its own KFAIL=20 window), seq, the swing width 19,
the activity scale axis (its own 28-bar lookback), the 5-bar dwell, and
every cut rule. All fitted quantities are re-estimated at each window on
the shipped fit window, because a score built on a different lookback
has a different distribution and reusing the old standardisation would
compare two things that are not comparable.

CONTROL: rebuilding at 106 reproduces the shipped states exactly, and
the run asserts it.

THE RESULT, AND IT IS NOT THE COMFORTABLE ONE. 0.806 agreement at 90
bars and 0.831 at 120, against 0.939-0.964 for the refit test. Moving
the lookback by 15% changes roughly ONE CALL IN SIX; re-estimating
every fitted number from a fit window seven years shorter changes one
in twenty. THE WINDOW IS A BIGGER LEVER THAN THE FITTING.

The disagreements are also a different KIND. Under refit the median
disagreement run was 3-4 bars, BELOW the 5-bar dwell, and 3-6% of
episodes were fully relabelled -- boundary noise. Here the median run
is 7 bars, ABOVE the dwell, and 15-16% of episodes are fully
relabelled. Changing the window does not jitter the edges; it tells a
different story about whole stretches of market.

WHAT THAT DOES AND DOES NOT MEAN. It is not a cliff: kappa is still
0.74 and 0.77, substantial agreement, and 120 sits closer to the
shipped read than 90 as a monotone drift rather than a discontinuity.
It does mean the lookback is a REAL CHOICE carrying real consequences,
not a free parameter -- which is an argument for leaving it locked and
documented rather than revisiting it, and an argument against reading
any single state call as though the window were incidental to it.

The fragile cells are the same ones refit exposed: trending and ranging
hold ~0.85 at both windows, while trend-in-range and neither fall to
0.73 at 90 bars. The overlap cell and the residual cell inherit the
wobble of both cuts.
