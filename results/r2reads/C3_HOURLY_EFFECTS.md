# C3 — RAW HOURLY EFFECTS

Generated 2026-11-24 10:36 by `code/c3hourly.py`.


**RAW EFFECTS ONLY. NOTHING TRADABLE IS BUILT HERE** — no signal, no entry rule, no book, and nothing wired into a strategy. That was the instruction.


## Method

- Hourly OANDA bid/ask for all 28 pairs; the mid is `(bid+ask)/2` and the move is its log difference in **basis points**.

- **Per currency, not per pair.** For `XXXYYY` a `+r` move is `+r` for XXX and `−r` for YYY, and each currency's figure is the mean of its signed legs across the seven pairs containing it — that currency against the basket of the other seven.

- Every figure is given **gross and net of that hour's own median spread**, measured from the same file. The net column subtracts **one** spread crossing, because a round trip crosses the spread once.

- **Measured on pre-2016 data and confirmed once on 2016–2020. 2021 onward stays sealed** and is never read here.

- `t_across_pairs` is the effect divided by its standard error across the seven pairs of that currency. It asks whether the hour is *consistent* across pairs, not whether it is tradable.
