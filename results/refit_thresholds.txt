How far each fitted parameter moves across vintages
===================================================

The 2015 row is the shipped fit and is the reference. mt and mc are the
two score cut points; act_cut_lo/hi are the activity tercile cuts
averaged across pairs; mean_* and sd_* are the zfit standardisation
statistics averaged across the 28 pairs.

refit_thresholds_relative.csv holds the same table as a fractional
change from the 2015 values.
