Driver C -- equity correlation separation
=========================================

Rolling |corr(pair returns, S&P returns)| at 21 and 63 bars, lagged one.
Absolute level: strength, not sign. Episode-based; crisis episodes are
those overlapping a forward-only window from an event date to +15 bars.

Null circularly shifts the S&P RETURN series against price, so the
index keeps its own behaviour and only the alignment breaks.
