Daily 10-year yield coverage
============================

FRED is daily for USD only. Every other G8 long-rate series
there is MONTHLY (IRLTLT01xxM156N, median gap 31 days), which
is useless against a daily FX panel. Checked before building,
because the 2-year file turned out to be missing NZD entirely.

A curve slope needs BOTH tenors. Available for USD, EUR.
AUD and CAD have a 2-year but no daily 10-year; NZD has
neither. The driver therefore runs on 1 of 28 pairs:
EURUSD
