Breadth -- how often is FX one market rather than 28?
=====================================================

Per day, how many of the 28 pairs share the modal state. The floor is
7, not 1: four states over 28 pairs means the largest group cannot be
smaller than ceil(28/4).

The widest decile is tested against the 54-event crisis calendar with a
circular-shift null on the breadth series, because "the widest days are
crisis days" is exactly the kind of claim that is true by construction
if crisis days are simply more common in some years.
